MathBlend 2.1.0

An educational mathematics board game for Windows.

MathBlend combines tile placement with mathematical equations to make practicing arithmetic interactive.

Version 2.1.0 includes:

  • Updated premium-square scoring: each premium square applies only on the first play that occupies it.
  • A premium square is restored if a successful challenge reverses the play that occupied it.
  • Corrected parenthesis staging behavior: staging an opening parenthesis from the rack does not consume or disable the closing parenthesis.
  • Corrected Type Equation symbol validation so symbols available in the rack are accepted, subject to grade-level restrictions.
  • Corrected Type Equation preview to preserve free-parenthesis availability from the beginning of the turn, even after the physical unlocking parenthesis has been used in the typed placement.
