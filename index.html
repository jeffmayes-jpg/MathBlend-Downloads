MathBlend 2.1.1

An educational mathematics board game for Windows.

MathBlend combines tile placement with mathematical equations to make practicing arithmetic interactive.

Version 2.1.1 includes:

  • Corrected free-parenthesis allowances to support multiple free counterparts based on the turn-start rack.
  • Corrected Type Equation processing to assign free parentheses before blank-tile substitutions.
  • Updated Type Equation to recognize duplicate physical parentheses in the rack.
